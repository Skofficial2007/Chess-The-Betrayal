# Known opening traps: positions where one natural-looking move loses material
# or gets mated, and what to play there instead.
#
# A record is the moves up to and including the move that sets the trap, then the
# losing move and a sound reply, then the trap's name:
#
#   <moves> | avoid=<move> best=<move> | <name>
#
# Every record is replayed through the real engine when this file is compiled, and
# both moves are checked for legality in the position actually reached, so a record
# that is a ply out of step fails the build rather than shipping.
#
# "avoid" is the fact a trap records and must be right. "best" is one sound reply
# out of several a position usually has, not the only good move -- two sources
# naming different ones are both correct, so the compiler keeps the first.
#
# A few records mark the move that walks INTO a trap rather than the one that
# finally loses, because that is the move worth not playing: after 4...Ng4 5.h3 in
# the Fishing Pole, White still has a good game, but only by finding that the
# natural recapture is the losing move. Where a source recorded the entry move and
# the actual blunder came later, the later one is used.
#
# Recompile after editing (Chess: The Betrayal/AI/Rebuild Trap Book) or the shipped
# asset goes stale.
