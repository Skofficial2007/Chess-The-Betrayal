# ===========================================================================
# Irregular Openings (offbeat first moves)
# ===========================================================================
#
# Answers to the thirteen first moves the rest of the book has nothing to say
# about. A club player opens 1.g3 or 1.Nc3 far more often than they reach move
# twenty of a Najdorf, and without these the AI is improvising from Black's very
# first reply.
#
# Every line here is weight 1 deliberately. A line teaches the engine the position
# it starts from as much as the answers that follow, so each of these also adds its
# first move to what the AI plays as White. At weight 1 the whole group is about
# one percent of its opening choices, which is the intent — give it an answer, not
# a new habit. Weighted like the main lines they sit beside, the AI would start
# opening 1.g4 several times a session.
